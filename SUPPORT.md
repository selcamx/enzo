# Getting Support for Enzo

Need help setting up Enzo, reporting a bug, or asking about your account? Here is the fastest place for each:

## Inside the Enzo app (fastest for bugs)

Choose **Help › Send Feedback…** in the macOS menu bar. It files an issue here automatically with your Enzo version and macOS details, sends sanitized local diagnostics to us privately, and lets you opt into being credited by first name.

## Private Support, Billing & Security

- **Account, billing, refunds & private questions:** Email [hello@tryenzo.ai](mailto:hello@tryenzo.ai). Please do not post email addresses, receipts, or personal transcripts in public GitHub issues.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** Please report privately following [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
